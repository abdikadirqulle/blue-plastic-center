# Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Frontend foundation

- Application shell, navigation, company switcher, command search
- Design tokens and reusable components
- Executive dashboard and responsive states
- Module route placeholders
- Mock repositories and shared domain types
- Authentication screen designs

## Phase 2 — Accounting core

- Company setup and fiscal periods
- Chart of accounts and account registers
- Journal entry workflow and posting rules
- Trial balance, profit and loss, and balance sheet
- Audit events, approvals, and period locking

## Phase 3 — Sales and receivables

- Customers and contacts
- Items and services
- Estimates, sales orders, invoices, receipts, and credit notes
- Customer payments, statements, and aging

## Phase 4 — Purchasing and payables

- Vendors and purchasing controls
- Purchase orders, receiving, bills, credits, and payments
- Approval workflows and three-way matching

## Phase 5 — Inventory and fulfillment

- Warehouses, bins, stock ledger, and costing
- Transfers, counts, serials, lots, and assemblies
- Pick, pack, ship, barcode, and reorder workflows

## Phase 6 — Banking and reconciliation

- Cash, bank, and mobile-money ledgers
- Statement import, matching, and reconciliation
- Treasury and cash-flow dashboards

## Phase 7 — Enterprise expansion

- Projects and job costing
- Fixed assets and depreciation
- Budgets and forecasting
- Payroll and HR
- Multi-company, intercompany, and consolidation
- Custom reporting and integrations

## Release discipline

Each phase requires acceptance criteria, permissions, audit behavior, tests,
accounting review, migration notes, and user documentation before completion.
